Data checks

Rows with missing order IDs, invalid dates or non-positive sales values are excluded and counted. Identical-looking rows are flagged but retained because they may be valid separate line items. Repeat rate counts identified customers with at least two distinct valid order IDs. The tool does not calculate profit or validate refunds. Do not upload a sensitive file to a shared computer.

Methods / transparent sources

How to interpret these numbers

Your CSV gives positive sales line value, distinct orders, average order value and identified customer repeat rate in the currency you choose. Optional product and region breakdowns and period comparisons use only rows in your file. A single currency is assumed across the file, and incomplete months may distort comparisons. It is analysed in your browser and is not sent to this static site. The campaign planner is a scenario using your assumptions, not a prediction.

The national market panel uses seasonally adjusted ONS internet sales statistics for Great Britain. Online share is a percentage of retail sales excluding fuel; average weekly online sales is in £ millions per week. It is separate from your chosen country and currency, and does not represent that country’s market. Data are refreshed only when a new ONS release is published and this site is rebuilt.

Source: ONS Retail Sales Index internet sales, series MS6Y and MZX6. See source code, tests and refresh instructions.
